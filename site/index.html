The workflow

Capture. Insert. Find.

01 / Capture

Keep the moment.

Record system audio, your microphone, or both from the menu bar. Transcribe as you record or after you finish.

  • Pause and resume a recording
  • Save Markdown with titles and labels

02 / Insert

Speak at the cursor.

Hold Right Command, speak, and release to insert text in a compatible app. If insertion fails, you can copy the result.

  • Custom shortcut with hold or toggle
  • Word replacements for names and phrases

03 / Find

Find the words again.

Find a recording by title or label, then search within its transcript. Copy the text or bring in an audio file to transcribe.

  • Copy plain text or Markdown
  • Follow the transcript as retained audio plays

Notes
Use the built-in offline outline, or configure a notes provider.
Remote notes
Your chosen provider receives transcript text to generate notes. Local-only mode blocks remote requests.
